Understanding your BMI

A quick guide to the adult BMI ranges.

UnderweightBelow 18.5
Normal weight18.5 – < 25
Overweight25 – < 30
Obesity30 and above

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

i
A guide, not a diagnosis. BMI is an adult screening estimate and doesn’t account for muscle mass or individual differences. It is not intended for children or pregnancy. Talk with a healthcare professional for personalized advice.
